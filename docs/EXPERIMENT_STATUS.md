## Metric conflicts and aggregation cautions

- The plan-reasoning report's 47.69%, 43.28%, and 48.21% values use different
  denominators: selected-plan executions, all-plan episodes, and episode
  hindsight. They are retained together; none replaces the others.
- The plan bank's four-step maximum and the Branch-A maximum-eight continuation
  are different controller contracts. A favorable result from either cannot be
  assigned to the other.
- Width-relative reflection passed several overall gates but failed the frozen
  beam-width-quartile stability clause. The overall 86.7% does not override
  that failure.
- Candidate transition comparisons report point estimates and confidence
  intervals but explicitly did not preregister a winner threshold. They are not
  promoted to a confirmed model-selection conclusion.
- The completed v13 protected confirmation concerns Branch-A numerical control.
  It must not be cited as completion of Qwen supervisor or counterfactual
  selector frozen evaluation.
